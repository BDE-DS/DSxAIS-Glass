01

Program overview

AIS Glass's marketing team owns strategy, campaign creation and agency briefing, while execution sits with external agencies. That split changes what this training needs to do. The team does not need ad platform mechanics, campaign setup, targeting or bidding. It needs to move faster and think sharper on the work that stays in-house, research, first draft content, campaign strategy and the briefs that go out to agencies.

This programme is built around that reality. Every module ties back to a piece of work the team already owns, research, decks, copy, creative direction, or the agency brief itself, and shows how AI shortens the distance between a raw idea and a finished, usable output.

What this programme covers

Five modules, in this exact order. AI fundamentals to level the whole team regardless of prior exposure, research and market intelligence for both the architectural and automotive sides of the business, content and creative to reduce first draft dependence on agencies, campaign strategy and agency briefing to sharpen the work that goes out the door, and a final module on memory and consistency so the gains hold across both sub teams after the session ends.

Who this is for

AIS Glass's 10 member internal marketing team, spanning the Gurgaon and Thane sub teams. No technical background required. Every exercise is built around real research, content and briefing work drawn from AIS's own architectural and automotive glass segments.

  • The C.R.A.F.T. prompting framework
  • Common beginner mistakes and how to avoid them
  • Live demo, same task run across tools

Team can confidently prompt any major AI tool and pick the right one for a given marketing task.
